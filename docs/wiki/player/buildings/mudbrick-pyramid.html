Brick-core (Mudbrick) Pyramid

Brick-core pyramids use a brick body with a finishing limestone casing (help id 55). Not mastabas (brick-only) and not stepped pyramids (plain stone, no polish). Guilds: Bricklayers, Stonemasons, Carpenters, and Work Camps.

Heaven chart totals (bricks / limestone): S 4800/192 · M 24k/432 · L 67.2k/768 · Complex 144.4k/1200. Per-phase delivery in Akhenaten still mirrors the true-pyramid schedule (soft fidelity).

Construction

  1. Clear / level (Work Camps).
  2. Brick courses + timber ramps (Bricklayers + Carpenters); limestone stocked for casing.
  3. Top-down limestone polish (Stonemasons) — no more bricks/limestone in the phase table.
  4. Finished monument; congrats mudbrick_pyramid_congratulations.
