Caesareum Cleo

The Caesareum is Alexandria’s seaside temple complex: a central marble temple, paved patio, surrounding portico, and two granite obelisks at the entrance. Build on clear land (15×15). Guilds: Work Camp (clear + haul), Stonemasons, and Carpenters (scaffolding). Not Artisans; materials are marble, timber, and late granite — not sandstone. The entrance obelisks are part of the finished monument art, not separate Obelisk buildings.

Placement

  • Entire footprint must be clear land (all green). No cliffs/rocks (unlike Pharos / Abu Simbel).
  • Only one unfinished Caesareum at a time (#one_caesareum_only).
  • Marble is not a place-gate; peasants haul it once ≥4 blocks sit in a single Storage Yard.

Construction

  • Work Camp peasants clear/level the site, then haul white marble (import).
  • Stonemasons build central temple → patio floor → portico columns/roofs.
  • Carpenters raise timber scaffolding during temple and portico phases.
  • Late phase: granite for the two entrance obelisks (quarry or import).
  • Visual order (GIF 20 frames): clear → temple (+scaffold) → patio → portico → gardens/obelisks.
